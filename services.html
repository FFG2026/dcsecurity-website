Home / Services

What we install, and why it's specified that way.

Four disciplines, one team: access control, intruder systems, commercial electrical and data cabling. Below is what's actually involved in each — not a marketing summary.

01 / Access control

Access control & gate automation

Controlling who gets into a building or site, and when, without turning it into a daily hassle for staff and visitors.

  • Automated gates and barriers, sized and speced to the vehicle traffic they'll actually see
  • Keypad, fob and card entry systems, including multi-door and multi-site setups
  • Integration with existing intercom or visitor management systems where needed
  • Scheduled access profiles for staff, contractors and out-of-hours visitors
02 / Intruder systems

Intruder alarms & CCTV

Systems designed to detect a problem early and give you a clear, actionable alert — not a box that just makes noise.

  • Monitored intruder alarms with remote signalling to a monitoring centre or your own team
  • CCTV systems covering perimeters, entry points and internal areas as required
  • Motion, contact and glass-break detection matched to how the building is used
  • Remote viewing and alert configuration on your phone or PC
03 / Electrical

Commercial electrical installations

Electrical work carried out to current regulations, whether that's a small upgrade or a full commercial fit-out.

  • New installations, rewires and upgrades for commercial premises
  • Distribution boards, containment and power circuits specified for the load
  • Testing, inspection and compliance certification
  • Fault-finding and reactive electrical work when something goes down
04 / Data cabling

Structured data cabling

The physical network most other systems depend on, installed cleanly and documented properly.

  • Structured cabling for data, voice and connected security systems
  • Comms room design, containment and patching
  • Testing and certification for every run installed
  • Moves, adds and changes as your site evolves
