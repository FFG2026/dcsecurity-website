Safety & compliance

Automatic gates are machinery. They should be treated that way.

An automated gate can cause serious injury if it isn't installed and maintained correctly. Whoever installs or takes over an automated gate takes on responsibility for its safety. We work to the recognised standards and leave you with the paperwork to show it.

  • Risk assessment for every installation
  • Safety edges, photocells and force limiting fitted where required
  • Impact force testing to BS EN 12453 and BS EN 12445 methods
  • Conformity documentation and a technical file at handover
  • Clear advice if an existing gate doesn't meet current safety expectations
Integration

Gates that work with the rest of your security.

Because we also install access control, CCTV and cabling, the gate doesn't end up as a stand-alone system someone else has to make work.

  • Audio and video intercoms, including GSM where there's no network on site
  • Keypads, fobs, cards and proximity readers with scheduled access
  • ANPR for vehicle recognition and logging
  • CCTV covering the entrance, with remote viewing

Common questions

Gate automation FAQ

How much does an automatic gate cost?

It depends on the gate type, size and weight, the ground and power supply, and how much access control is integrated. We survey the site and give an itemised quote, so you can see what's included before you commit.

Can you automate my existing gates?

Often, yes. We assess the gate's condition, hinges or track, structure and weight first. If it's sound, we can fit operators and safety devices to it. If it isn't, we'll tell you what needs repairing or replacing.

How often should automatic gates be serviced?

Regular servicing keeps gates safe and reliable. How often depends on the gate, how busy it is and the manufacturer's guidance. Busy commercial sites typically need more frequent visits than lightly used ones.

What happens if my gate breaks down?

Call us on 01634 715 690. We'll diagnose the fault, repair or replace the failed part, and check the safety devices before the gate goes back into use.

Can the gates link to our access control and CCTV?

Yes. Gates can work with intercoms, keypads, fobs, cards, ANPR and CCTV, so entry, vehicle access and monitoring run as one system rather than separate ones.
