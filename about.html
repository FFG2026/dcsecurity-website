Home / About

One team, across four disciplines.

Direct Commercial Security was set up so commercial sites didn't have to juggle separate contractors for alarms, access, electrics and cabling.

We're based at Upper Upnor, just outside Rochester in Kent, and work on commercial sites across the South of England — from single-site alarm and access upgrades to full electrical and cabling fit-outs run alongside security installs.

The idea behind the business is simple: specify properly, install it right the first time, and be the single point of contact when something needs looking at afterwards. That means one team turning up, not three different subcontractors passing the job between them.
